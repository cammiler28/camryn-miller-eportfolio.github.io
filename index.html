About Me

I am a junior majoring in Media Production with a minor in Management, combining creative storytelling with strategic thinking and business insight. I began my professional journey as a Digital Marketing Intern and advanced into the role of Digital Marketing & Social Media Specialist, where I now oversee content creation, brand development, and digital communications for a professional brokerage firm.

My experience spans video production and editing in Adobe Premiere Pro, web development, content strategy, social media management, and digital infrastructure development. I have hands-on experience working with JVC and DSLR camera systems, as well as professional audio setups including boom, handheld, and lavalier microphones. I also have experience with podcast production and audio recording, allowing me to create high-quality, well-produced multimedia content across platforms.

I enjoy building cohesive brand systems, designing strong visual identities, and translating business goals into impactful digital media.

Originally from Lynchburg, Virginia, and having lived in Indianapolis and Syracuse, I bring a diverse perspective to storytelling and design. Outside of professional work, I am passionate about creative media, brand aesthetics, and continuous learning. My goal is to grow as a creative strategist who blends artistic vision with structured marketing execution.
